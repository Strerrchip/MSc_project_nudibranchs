Species-level variation in colour-pattern metrics derived from 295 online images of 30 nudibranch species. (A) Six animal-only metrics used to describe internal pattern boldness. (B) Nine contrasts between each animal and its immediate environment used to describe detectability. Each cell shows a species mean standardised across the 30 species; red values are above the among-species mean and blue values are below it. Species are ordered alphabetically.
