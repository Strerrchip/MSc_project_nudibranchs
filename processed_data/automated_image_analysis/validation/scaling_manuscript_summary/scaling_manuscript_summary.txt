Final scaling summary for the manuscript
==========================================

The rounded median scaling target was 3,629 pixels.
Of 295 retained images, 147 animals above the target were downscaled and 148 images at or below the target were retained at their original resolution.
The 147 strictly below-target animal perimeters ranged from 725 to 3,622 pixels (20.0-99.8% of the target).
No image was enlarged (maximum scale factor = 1.000).
