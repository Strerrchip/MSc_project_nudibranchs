Van background external comparison
==================================

Overlapping species: 9
Viewing distances: 2 cm and 30 cm
Metric pairs: 4
Total comparisons: 8

Statistical approach:
Pearson and Spearman correlations were calculated using species-level z-scores.
Benjamini-Hochberg correction was applied across the eight comparisons separately for Pearson and Spearman tests.

Important interpretation:
The paired metrics are conceptually related but not mathematically identical.
The online-image metrics use uncalibrated photographs and CIELAB edge approximations.
The van den Berg metrics use calibrated photographs, triggerfish vision and QCPA.

Largest absolute Spearman association:
luminance_edge_cv at 30 cm: rho = 0.750, raw p = 0.0199, BH q = 0.1595
