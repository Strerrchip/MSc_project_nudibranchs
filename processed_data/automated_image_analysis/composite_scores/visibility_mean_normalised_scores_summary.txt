Mean normalised visibility scores
=================================

Species: 30
Images represented: 295
Boldness metrics averaged: 6
Detectability metrics averaged: 9

Method:
Each species-level metric was z-standardised across the 30 species using the sample standard deviation.
All metrics were already oriented so that larger values represented greater boldness or detectability.
The standardised metrics were averaged separately for boldness and detectability.
The existing PCA outputs were not changed and remain available as secondary analyses.

Mean normalised boldness range: -1.1710 to 0.8696
Mean normalised detectability range: -1.1755 to 0.7323
