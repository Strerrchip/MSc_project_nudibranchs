Example of the image segmentation and background-sampling workflow. (A) Scaled online image; (B) final animal mask shown in red; (C) the surrounding 120-pixel background ring shown in green; and (D) the two analysis regions combined. The example shows Aphelodoris varia (Aphelodoris_varia_img03), with the mask obtained from automatic_qc_pass. The animal perimeter before scaling was 4077.37 pixels, compared with the median target perimeter of 3629.35 pixels. The applied scale factor was 0.890, giving a final perimeter of 3612.36 pixels. Scale factors were capped at 1, so images below the target size were not enlarged. Background pixels were sampled within 120 pixels of the animal mask.
