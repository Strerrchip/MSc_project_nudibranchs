Relationships between species-level mean normalised colour-pattern scores and continuous chemical-defence strength. (A) unpalatability and boldness; (B) unpalatability and detectability; (C) toxicity and boldness; and (D) toxicity and detectability. Each point represents one species, and solid lines show ordinary least-squares fits. Pearson and Spearman tests were corrected separately across the four planned relationships using the Benjamini-Hochberg method. The asterisk marks a relationship with q < 0.05 after correction.
