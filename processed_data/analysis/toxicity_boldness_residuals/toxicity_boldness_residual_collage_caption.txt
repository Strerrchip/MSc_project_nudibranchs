Examples from the four species with the largest absolute internally studentised residuals in the toxicity-boldness relationship. Rows show species in descending residual rank. Columns show images with lower, median and higher image-level boldness within each species, selected reproducibly from the six animal-only metrics. The scatter-plot analysis used species means; these images illustrate within-species photographic variation.
