Relationships between mean normalised colour-pattern scores and chemical defence across nudibranch species. Panels show unpalatability with boldness (A) and detectability (B), and toxicity with boldness (C) and detectability (D). Each point represents one species, and solid lines show ordinary least-squares regressions. Pearson correlations were used as the primary tests, while Spearman correlations were used as robustness checks. Benjamini-Hochberg q values were calculated across the four tests.
